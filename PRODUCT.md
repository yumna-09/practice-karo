# Digital Dost — Product Brief

## Purpose
Help parents, grandparents, and adults with low digital confidence learn everyday technology through safe, patient, hands-on practice.

## Audience
Adults who may feel uncertain about smartphones, messaging, links, photos, and online safety. Do not assume technical knowledge or use patronising language.

## Product promise
Learn technology. Practise safely. Stay independent.

## Core principles
1. Teach one task at a time.
2. Explain what an action does before asking the learner to perform it.
3. Encourage practice and repetition without judgement.
4. Keep all MVP exercises simulated.
5. Be transparent about what is local, predefined, device-dependent, or not implemented.

## MVP areas
- Learning home with clear task entry points.
- Guided digital companion with contextual, step-based help.
- Simulated messaging practice, including local-only image preview.
- Scam Detective with fictional examples and explanations.
- Local learning progress.
- English-first interface.
- Accessibility preferences: text size, contrast, theme, reduced motion, and optional read-aloud.

## Out of scope for the current MVP
- Real WhatsApp or other messaging integrations.
- Banking transactions or real financial accounts.
- Live analysis of private messages or attachments.
- Login, cloud accounts, and cross-device synchronisation.
- Claims of fully translated Roman Urdu speech without a verified translation service.
- Live AI model integration unless a safe, affordable, privacy-reviewed service is deliberately selected.

## Companion behaviour
The companion should be task-oriented rather than an open-ended generic chatbot. Prefer guided topics, numbered steps, safety reminders, and clear fallback language. Clearly disclose that current responses are predefined local guidance.

## Success criteria
- A first-time learner can find a practice task without assistance.
- Each practice task explains that it is simulated.
- The learner can complete a task and understand the feedback.
- Scam examples explain the warning signs and a safe next action.
- Text is legible, controls are keyboard accessible, and settings work on desktop and mobile.
- The product never implies that a real message, payment, or account action occurred.
